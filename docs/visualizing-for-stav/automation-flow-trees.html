Automation & Decision Flow Trees

Every branch of the full funnel — from first inquiry to post-trip community — with each automation, each message, and each point where an alert fires to Stav or a human decision replaces an automatic one. The payment spine is card tokenization: the card is tokenized at registration, the real charge runs ~14 days before departure.

1

First inquiry — lead capture & the 2-hour SLA

Every channel (site form, Facebook, WhatsApp) drops a lead into one inbox. Nothing is allowed to sit unanswered — an immediate auto-reply goes out, and if no one has called back in 2 hours the lead escalates. Anti-spam already blocks automated form fills.

  • Inquiry arrives — site form / Facebook / WhatsApp / private-trip form
    Captured and classified in one place.
    Customer→lead
    • Immediate auto-reply (WhatsApp)
      "We got your inquiry — Stav will get back to you shortly."
      System→Customer
    • Auto-WhatsApp to pre-qualify (not a full chatbot)
      Asks for participants, date, trip type — filters serious inquiries before Stav calls back.
      System→Customer
    • Has Stav called back? (timer)
      Time
      • called within 2h
        Move lead to "needs clarification" → Flow 2
        go to Flow 2 ↓
      • 2h passed, no contact
        Second alert to Stav + move to "urgent handling"
        System→Stav

Volume today is low (a few a day), so the auto-reply + pre-qualify is what carries this stage; the 2-hour escalation is the safety net that stops a lead from being forgotten.

2

Discovery & needs — the mandatory-fields gate

The discovery call collects the details a quote depends on. The system blocks the lead from advancing — and blocks any quote from being sent — until the required fields are filled: date, number of participants, trip type, budget.

  • Lead is in "needs clarification"
    • Reminder to schedule the discovery call
      System→Stav
    • Discovery call — capture group size, date, difficulty, budget, expectations
      Stav
      • required fields missing
        Block stage transition — no quote can be sent
        Hard gate on date + participants + type + budget.
        System
      • all required fields filled
        Unlock quote generation → Flow 3
        go to Flow 3 ↓
3

Price quote & closing — 24h / 48h reminders

The quote goes out over email and WhatsApp with a dynamic link. Two automatic reminders chase it; if it's still open after 48 hours, an urgent phone-follow-up task lands in Stav's calendar so the lead doesn't cool off.

  • Send the quote (email + WhatsApp) with a dynamic link
    System→Customer
    • +24h, no response
      First reminder (WhatsApp)
      System→Customer
    • +48h, no response
      Second reminder (WhatsApp)
      System→Customer
      • Still no response → open "urgent phone follow-up" task in Stav's calendar
        System→Stav
    • customer approves the quote
      Advance to registration & card → Flow 4
      go to Flow 4 ↓
4

Registration & card — tokenize, don't charge

The seat is secured by tokenizing the card, not by paying (#46). Cardcom stores the card and returns a Token; the site keeps only the Token. Booking becomes reserved — seat secured, capacity occupied, no money moved.

  • Customer registers for a departure
    Picks participants; a separate marketing opt-in checkbox is required to join the mailing list (§2).
    Customer
    • Seat held & customer redirected to Cardcom
      A reassurance message shows first ("you're moving to a secure payment environment").
      System→heldHOLD ≈ 40 min · #44
      • Cardcom runs a tokenize-only operation
        No charge and no credit-limit hold. Card stored encrypted at Cardcom; a Token is returned.
        Cardcom
        • token created
          Save the Token only — never the card number
          Token stays valid until the card itself expires — no time limit.
          System→reserved#46
          • Booking confirmation (email + WhatsApp)
            Must NOT say "we saved your card" — Cardcom holds the card, we hold only the Token (#48). No receipt yet — the receipt is issued only when money moves.
            System→CustomerthenFlow 5 ↓
        • fail · abandon · hold expires
          Seat released, booking expires
          Nothing was charged, so nothing to refund. "Paid but no seat" is designed out (#32).
          System→expired
5

The charge — fires ~14 days before departure

Trigger time = max(departure − 14 days, now) (#47). A departure booked a month out is charged at D−14; one booked inside 14 days is charged ≈ immediately — same path. This is the only place money moves, and where the receipt is issued (#48).

  • Time trigger: max(departure − 14d, now)
    Timeonreserved
    • Charge the full amount to the saved Token
      Cardcom captures it like a normal online purchase.
      System→Cardcom
      • charge succeeds
        Booking becomes paid & confirmed
        System→confirmed#47
        • Cardcom auto-issues the receipt (חשבונית מס/קבלה) & emails it
          Via the Document block, folded into the charge call — only now, because money moved (#36, #48).
          Cardcom→Customer
      • charge fails
        (a) Auto-retry the Token
        A transient decline may clear on retry.
        System#49
        • still failing
          (b) Send the customer a payment link
          Self-serve fix — the customer pays through a fresh link.
          System→Customer
        • (c) Alert Stav: retries failed, link sent
          System→Stav
        • still unpaid after grace window
          (d) Auto-cancel & release the seat
          The only automatic cancellation in the system — distinct from the under-minimum decision, which is always Stav's button (Flow 9).
          System→cancelled#49

Why 14 days: Cardcom can't hold a credit-limit reservation for more than a week, so instead of authorizing early we tokenize now and capture near departure. The charge window (D−14) and the whole cancellation-fee matrix live inside it.

6

Trip preparation — the countdown messages & guide checklist

As the date approaches, a fixed sequence of messages keeps the customer ready and the guide equipped. Stav sets the exact number of reminders and their timings.

  • Departure date approaching · booking is confirmed
    Time
    • 7 days before
      Admin letter: gear list, meeting point + schedule, Waze
      System→Customer
    • 3 days before
      Reminder + clear cancellation clause
      States plainly that cancelling now carries a fee (see Flow 8).
      System→Customer
    • evening before
      Waze link + guide's phone (+ optional weather forecast)
      With multiple guides later, the system pulls the guide assigned to this trip.
      System→Customer
    • Customer's digital arrival confirmation
      Customer→System
    • Guide checklist — verify gear & logistics
      System→Guide
7

Follow-up & community — after the trip

Close the loop: collect anything outstanding, gather reviews and referrals, and fold happy hikers into the community. Adding someone to the mailing list requires their explicit opt-in (§2).

  • Trip has ended
    Time
    • day / evening after
      Social links — upload photos, share the experience
      A discount-for-share incentive is planned as a later add-on.
      System→Customer
    • 24h after
      Collect any outstanding balance
      Applies only if a trip was sold on a deposit basis; a full charge at D−14 leaves nothing to collect. If a balance exists, a collection task is opened.
      System
    • Satisfaction survey (NPS) + Google/Facebook review link
      System→Customer
    • Add to the hikers' community & mailing list
      Only with opt-in consent. Customer profile becomes a "community member."
      System
8

Cancellation & refund — decided by charge state, not the calendar

The first fork is has the charge happened yet (#50). Before the charge nothing was taken, so cancelling is free for everyone. After the charge, the fork is who cancels. All refunds are issued manually by Stav from the dashboard (#15) — there is no customer self-serve refund.

  • A booking is being cancelled
    Fork on charge state ↓
    • before the charge · >14 days out · still reserved
      Release seat + drop the Token — nothing owed
      No money ever moved, so there is no refund. The clean zone (whoever cancels).
      System→cancelled
    • after the charge · money already taken
      Fork on who cancels ↓
      • customer cancels
        Refund minus the policy fee, by tier (#13)
        Stav issues it manually; all tiers fall inside the 14-day charged window. Stav finalizes the exact numbers.
        Stav→Customer
        • Cancel 5–6 days before → fee 50 ₪ / participant
        • Cancel 3–4 days before → fee 150 ₪ / participant
        • Cancel within 48 hours → full cost / participant, no refund
      • company cancels — weather · below minimum · force majeure
        Full refund OR a free reschedule to a new date
        No fee. This is the branch behind the "cancel trip" button (Flow 9) and mid-trip weather cancels (§6).
        Stav→Customer

Cardcom refund fee: to confirm whether Cardcom charges a fee per credit (e.g. ~10 ₪). If it does, the cancellation message must disclose that the refund is net of a handling fee (§1).

9

Under-minimum escalation — staged alerts to Stav

When a departure is below its minimum, the system nudges Stav on a schedule so he can act while it's still cheap to cancel. The final cancel is always Stav's button — a departure is never auto-cancelled for low enrollment (#52). The charge still fires automatically.

  • Departure is below its minimum participants
    Stav sets the min per trip; he defines the alert timings/frequency.
    System
    • ≈ D−21 · a week before the charge
      Rung 1 — notice to Stav before the charge
      Placed in the "before charge = nothing owed, no refunds" zone so a cancel here is clean.
      System→Stav
      • Stav tries to save the departure
        Recruit more registrants · push to cabins & social · move the date.
        Stav
    • further rungs · e.g. D−7, D−2
      Repeat escalation alerts to Stav
      The charge fires automatically in between — so later cancels land after money moved.
      System→Stav
    • Stav decides to cancel — clicks "Cancel trip"
      Cancel-trip button (bulk) — never automatic
      Stav→cancelled#53
      • Cancels all bookings + auto-refunds the charged ones
        Company-cancel branch of Flow 8 → full refund to all.
        System→Customer
      • Sends the legal cancellation message
        "Trip cancelled per the cancellation policy — the required minimum wasn't met; your payment is refunded in full."
        System→Customer
10

Admin action set — Stav can intervene at any stage

The manual override surface (#53), usable at every stage, including after the trip has run (e.g. rain starts mid-trip → cancel and refund everyone, §6). These are buttons, not automations.

  • Admin dashboard — Stav
    Stav
    • Edit trail / departure fields
      Dates, capacity, minimum, price, time.
    • Open / close registration
      closed = sales stopped but the trip still runs (e.g. up to trip start time). Distinct from cancelled.
    • Cancel trip (bulk)
      → Flow 9 tail: refund all + legal message.
    • Add a late participant
      Join a departure after it's "closed" / already on the calendar (#51).
    • Create a private trip (phone-closed deal)
      Template: contact name + phone, date, location/type, participants, price, price per extra participant (e.g. >7 → fixed surcharge each).
      • System generates a Cardcom payment link + a trip page
        The page carries the trip details and the cancellation terms.
        System→Customer
    • Admin-cancel a single booking
      → refund per the Flow 8 matrix.
    • Reschedule a booking to another departure
      Old seat released, new seat taken; the free-reschedule branch of Flow 8.
    • Contact a customer
